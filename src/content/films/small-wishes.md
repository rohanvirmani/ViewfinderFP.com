*An ambitious young woman trapped in an archaic village decides to shed the
limitations of her coveted long hair for a chance at a life on her own terms.*

"Small Wishes" is VFP's second original production, co-directed by Santhosh Jay
and Sabareesh Subramaniam, recipients of the second edition of the Iyackuddy
Rabindran Short Film Fund. The Tamil-language short celebrates women's
empowerment and the importance of education for women, using a simple yet
powerful story to inspire change. It has gone on to screen at prominent
international film festivals and achieve viral success online, furthering
VFP's mission to champion impactful South Asian storytelling.
