"Dinner Party" is VFP's fourth original production, filmed in Toronto, directed
by Sabareesh Subramaniam and written by BC-based Payal Raj. The heartfelt short
follows a young South Asian Canadian couple as they prepare a Diwali dinner for
their families, only to have the evening unravel when a disagreement about
starting a family comes to light. The film has screened at three international
film festivals, continuing VFP's mission to tell authentic, culturally rooted
stories that resonate with diverse audiences.
