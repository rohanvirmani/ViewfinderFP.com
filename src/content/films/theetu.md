> One does not forget their caste, even when buying carrots.

In 2020, Viewfinder Film Productions (VFP) selected filmmaker Haresh Narayanan
as the first recipient of the Iyackuddy Rabindran Short Film Fund, created in
honour of VFP founder Dilani's late father, a lifelong supporter of her passion
for cinema. This collaboration resulted in *Untouchability* (*Theetu*), VFP's
first original production — a bold Tamil-language short exploring pressing
social themes. The film premiered at prestigious festivals such as the UK Asian
Film Festival, New York Indian Film Festival, and South Asian Film Festival of
America, earning finalist recognition at the Swedish International Film
Festival. Theetu set the foundation for VFP's growth into producing globally
celebrated South Asian stories.
