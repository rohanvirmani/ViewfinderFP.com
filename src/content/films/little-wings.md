"Little Wings" is VFP's third original production, directed by Naveen Mu. The
Tamil-language short follows an elderly woman trapped in a bitter marriage who
is pushed to the brink when her paralyzed husband sets out to consume her pet
rooster. The film won multiple Best Film awards on the festival circuit, earned
five Critics Choice Award nominations, and was the only Tamil short selected
for the Indian Panorama at the 53rd International Film Festival of India. Its
crowning achievement is winning the 71st Indian National Film Award for Best
Cinematography (Non-Feature) in 2025 — a landmark honour that firmly established
VFP's reputation in international independent cinema.

The film was released by prominent Indian production and distribution company,
Dream Warrior Pictures, on February 27, 2026.
